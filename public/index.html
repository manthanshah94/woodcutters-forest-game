SABHA LEADERBOARD
❦
Vachanamrut · Gadhada Pratham 18

The Sheikh knew.
The woodcutter didn't.

Every log you chopped was sandalwood — a forest gifted by the King, precious beyond what the woodcutter ever imagined. He burned it to charcoal and sold it cheap. A whole year passed. He stayed poor.

The Sheikh appeared and offered triple the price — not for charcoal, but for the wood as it was. Unburned. Unaltered. Still carrying its true worth.

Swamiji's message in Pratham 18 is this: the manushya deh — this human life — is that forest. Infinite potential lives inside it. And yet, minute by minute, we burn it into charcoal: distraction, comfort, small pursuits, the noise of days that pass without recognition.

What are you holding that you haven't recognized yet?
